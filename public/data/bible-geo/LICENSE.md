# Bible map data — sources and licenses

Generated by `scripts/build-bible-geo.mjs`. These files are adapted from:

- **Places, coordinates, and confidence scores** — [OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data](https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Place descriptions and disambiguated names** — [Theographic Bible Metadata](https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Descriptions are from Easton's Bible Dictionary (1897, public domain).
- **Basemap (land, lakes, rivers)** — [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), public domain.

Changes: places were reduced to their highest-confidence identification, indexed by book/chapter/verse, and descriptions were shortened; the basemap was clipped to the biblical world and simplified.

Because the place files include CC BY-SA material, `places/*.json` are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
