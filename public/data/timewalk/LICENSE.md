# TimeWalk data — sources and licenses

Generated by `scripts/build-timewalk.mjs` and `scripts/fetch-timewalk-images.mjs`.

- **Events, people, family relationships, and per-verse links** — [Theographic Bible Metadata](https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata), licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Biographies are from Easton's Bible Dictionary (1897, public domain). Theographic's dates follow Archbishop James Ussher's chronology; WL labels them "Traditional" and shows approximate scholarly dates alongside.
- **Place photos** — selected via [OpenBible.info Bible Geocoding Data](https://github.com/openbibleinfo/Bible-Geocoding-Data) (CC BY 4.0). The photos themselves are hosted on Wikimedia Commons and are **not copied** into this project; each is shown with its author, license, and a link to its Commons page. Only public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, and Free Art License images are used (no GFDL-only, non-commercial, no-derivatives, or "all rights reserved" images).
- **Archaeology photos** (`find-images.json`) — found on Wikimedia Commons; license confirmed from each file's Commons metadata, with the same rules as above.

Changes made: Theographic records were reshaped into compact per-chapter and per-person files, references converted to STEPBible book codes, and biographies shortened.

Because these files include CC BY-SA material, `events.json`, `people*`, and `chapters/*` are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
