# The Quiet Seeker Curriculum

**A Contemplative Formation Program for the Reflective New Believer**

*Designed for those who are new to faith, learn best through reading and reflection, prefer personal study, and are seeking a deeper relationship with God.*

---

## About This Curriculum

You are **The Quiet Seeker** — someone drawn to faith through the still, small voice rather than the roar of the crowd. You learn best in silence with an open book. You prefer the intimacy of personal study to the bustle of group discussion. And above all else, you long to truly *know* God — not just know about Him.

This curriculum honors your contemplative nature while gently building the foundations of faith. Every course is designed for reflective, self-paced study with rich reading materials and space for personal meditation. While community will be introduced gradually, the emphasis remains on cultivating an intimate, personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

**Your Learning Style:** Reading, reflection, journaling, and meditation
**Your Preferred Setting:** Quiet, personal study
**Your Primary Goal:** Deep, intimate relationship with God
**Your Pace:** Self-directed, contemplative, unhurried

---

## Foundation Year: Meeting God

*The first year focuses on establishing the basics of faith through personal encounter with God and His Word. Each module is designed for quiet, reflective study.*

---

### Module 1: First Encounters (Months 1–2)

#### Understanding Your New Life

- **What Has Happened to Me?** — Understanding conversion, new birth, and your new identity
- **The Story of God's Love** — The gospel narrative from creation to redemption
- **Grace: The Heart of Christianity** — Understanding unmerited favor and what makes faith different
- **Assurance and Security** — Knowing you belong to God and nothing can separate you from His love
- **The Inner Witness** — How the Holy Spirit confirms your relationship with God

#### Beginning to Read Scripture

- **The Bible: God's Love Letter** — What Scripture is and why it matters for relationship with God
- **How the Bible Is Organized** — A gentle tour through the library of Scripture
- **Finding Your Way Around** — Basic navigation, translations, and choosing where to start
- **Reading for Relationship** — Approaching Scripture as conversation with God, not information gathering
- **The Gospel of John: Your First Book** — A guided introduction to the most relational Gospel

#### Recommended Reading
- *Mere Christianity* — C.S. Lewis
- *What's So Amazing About Grace?* — Philip Yancey
- *The Gospel of John* (read slowly, one chapter per day)

#### Reflective Practices
- Daily reading in John with journaling
- Writing letters to God about what you're discovering
- Noting questions that arise without needing immediate answers

---

### Module 2: Learning to Pray (Months 3–4)

#### Prayer as Conversation

- **What Is Prayer?** — Conversation with the God who listens
- **You Can Talk to God** — Permission to be honest, raw, and real
- **Listening Prayer** — The contemplative practice of hearing God's voice
- **The Lord's Prayer: A Template** — Jesus' model for intimate communication with the Father
- **When You Don't Know What to Say** — Praying through silence, groans, and wordlessness

#### Types of Prayer

- **Adoration** — Worshiping God for who He is
- **Confession** — The cleansing practice of honesty before God
- **Thanksgiving** — Cultivating gratitude as a way of life
- **Supplication** — Bringing your requests and the needs of others
- **Intercession** — Standing in the gap for others
- **Contemplation** — Resting in God's presence without words

#### Developing a Prayer Life

- **Creating Sacred Space** — Setting up your quiet place for meeting with God
- **Establishing Rhythm** — Finding sustainable patterns for daily prayer
- **Prayer Journaling** — Recording your conversations with God
- **Praying Through Scripture** — Letting God's Word shape your prayers
- **The Examen** — A daily practice for noticing God's presence

#### Recommended Reading
- *Prayer: Experiencing Awe and Intimacy with God* — Timothy Keller
- *A Praying Life* — Paul Miller
- *Beginning to Pray* — Anthony Bloom
- *Too Busy Not to Pray* — Bill Hybels

#### Reflective Practices
- Establishing a daily quiet time (start with 15–20 minutes)
- Keeping a prayer journal
- Practicing the examen each evening
- One extended time of silence per week (30–60 minutes)

- **The Son Who Accomplishes** — Jesus' work of salvation on your behalf

- *Knowing God* — J.I. Packer

- Meditating on one attribute of God per week

- Practicing calling God "Father" or "Abba" in prayer

## Practical Requirements

While your journey is personal and self-paced, these practices ensure balanced growth:

- **Daily Quiet Time** — Minimum 20–30 minutes of Scripture reading and prayer
- **Weekly Extended Time** — 1–2 hours for deeper study and contemplation
- **Monthly Accountability** — Check-in with a mentor, spiritual director, or trusted friend
- **Quarterly Retreat** — Half or full day in extended silence and prayer
- **Annual Extended Retreat** — 2–7 days of focused time with God
- **Reading** — Minimum 12 books per year from recommended lists
- **Journaling** — Regular written reflection on your journey
- **Scripture Memory** — Ongoing memorization of key passages
- **Church Participation** — Regular (if quiet) engagement with a local congregation
- **One Relationship** — At least one person walking closely with you in faith

## A Final Word

Dear Quiet Seeker,

Your path to God will look different from the extrovert who finds faith in community and conversation. And that's exactly as God designed it. He made you to meet Him in stillness, in pages, in the sacred silence of a soul undistracted.

Don't let anyone tell you that you need to become someone else to grow in Christ. Your quiet way is a gift — to yourself, to the church, and to a noisy world that desperately needs people who have been with God in the hidden place.
