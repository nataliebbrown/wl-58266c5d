# Biblical and Theological Studies Curriculum

A comprehensive seminary-style degree program for students pursuing advanced study in Scripture, theology, and ministry.

---

## Foundation Year

### Biblical Languages

- **Hebrew I & II** – Introduction to biblical Hebrew grammar, vocabulary, and syntax
- **Greek I & II** – Koine Greek fundamentals for New Testament study
- **Hebrew Exegesis** – Reading and translating Hebrew texts
- **Greek Exegesis** – Reading and translating Greek texts

### Biblical Studies Core

- **Old Testament Survey** – Genesis through Malachi, historical context and literary structure
- **New Testament Survey** – Gospels through Revelation, authorship and themes
- **Hermeneutics** – Principles of biblical interpretation
- **Biblical Geography and Archaeology** – Physical and cultural setting of Scripture

### Theological Foundations

- **Introduction to Theology** – Methodology and major theological categories
- **Spiritual Formation** – Personal devotion, prayer, and character development

---

## Intermediate Year

### Old Testament Studies

- **Pentateuch** – Genesis through Deuteronomy in depth
- **Historical Books** – Joshua through Esther
- **Wisdom Literature** – Job, Psalms, Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, Song of Solomon
- **Major Prophets** – Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, Daniel
- **Minor Prophets** – Hosea through Malachi

### New Testament Studies

- **Synoptic Gospels** – Matthew, Mark, and Luke
- **Gospel of John** – Theology and structure of the Fourth Gospel
- **Acts and Early Church** – History of apostolic Christianity
- **Pauline Epistles I** – Romans, Galatians, 1 & 2 Corinthians
- **Pauline Epistles II** – Ephesians, Philippians, Colossians, 1 & 2 Thessalonians, Pastoral Epistles, Philemon
- **General Epistles** – Hebrews, James, 1 & 2 Peter, 1-3 John, Jude
- **Revelation and Apocalyptic Literature**

### Systematic Theology Sequence

- **Prolegomena and Bibliology** – Theological method, revelation, and the doctrine of Scripture
- **Theology Proper** – The existence, attributes, and works of God
- **Trinitarianism** – Historical development and biblical basis for Trinitarian doctrine
- **Christology** – The person and work of Christ
- **Pneumatology** – The person and work of the Holy Spirit
- **Angelology and Demonology** – Angels, Satan, and spiritual warfare
- **Anthropology and Hamartiology** – Humanity and sin
- **Soteriology** – The doctrine of salvation
- **Ecclesiology** – The nature, purpose, and ordinances of the church
- **Eschatology** – Death, resurrection, judgment, and final things

---

## Advanced Year

### Historical Theology

- **Patristics** – Church fathers from the apostolic age through Augustine
- **Medieval Theology** – Scholasticism, monasticism, and pre-Reformation developments
- **Reformation Theology** – Luther, Calvin, Zwingli, the Anabaptists, and the Catholic Counter-Reformation
- **Modern Theology** – Enlightenment through contemporary movements
- **American Religious History** – Christianity in the American context

### Philosophy and Apologetics

- **Philosophy of Religion** – Arguments for God's existence, problem of evil, faith and reason
- **Christian Apologetics** – Defending the faith in contemporary contexts
- **Christianity and World Religions** – Comparative study of major religious traditions
- **Ethics** – Biblical and theological foundations for moral decision-making
- **Bioethics** – Beginning and end of life issues, medical ethics

### Contextual and Practical Studies

- **Homiletics I** – Principles of sermon preparation
- **Homiletics II** – Sermon delivery and advanced preaching methods
- **Pastoral Theology** – Shepherding, counseling, and church leadership
- **Christian Education** – Teaching methods and discipleship models
- **Missions and Cross-Cultural Ministry** – History, theology, and practice of global mission
- **Urban Ministry** – Ministry in multicultural and metropolitan contexts
- **Worship and Liturgy** – Theology and practice of Christian worship

---

## Electives and Specializations

### Advanced Biblical Studies

- **Intertestamental Period** – Second Temple Judaism and the world between the testaments
- **Dead Sea Scrolls**
- **New Testament Textual Criticism**
- **Old Testament Textual Criticism**
- **Biblical Theology** – Tracing themes across the canon
- **Aramaic** – For Daniel, Ezra, and related texts
- **Septuagint Studies** – The Greek Old Testament

### Specialized Theological Topics

- **Covenant Theology**
- **Dispensationalism**
- **Theology of the Reformers**
- **Eastern Orthodox Theology**
- **Catholic Theology**
- **Liberation Theology**
- **Feminist and Womanist Theology**
- **Theology of Suffering**
- **Theology of Work and Vocation**

### Ministry Specializations

- **Pastoral Counseling**
- **Marriage and Family Ministry**
- **Youth and Campus Ministry**
- **Children's Ministry**
- **Church Planting**
- **Church Administration and Leadership**
- **Nonprofit and Parachurch Ministry**
- **Chaplaincy** – Hospital, military, prison, or corporate settings

### Academic Track

- **Research Methods in Theology**
- **Academic Writing Seminar**
- **Independent Study**
- **Thesis or Capstone Project**

---

## Practical Requirements

Most seminary programs also require:

- **Supervised Ministry Internship** – Field experience in a church or ministry setting
- **Chapel Attendance** – Regular participation in community worship
- **Spiritual Direction or Mentorship** – Ongoing relationship with a faculty advisor
- **Oral Comprehensive Exams** – Demonstrating mastery of core theological disciplines
- **Thesis or Final Integrative Project**

---

## Notes

This curriculum reflects a fairly traditional Protestant seminary structure. Catholic, Orthodox, or denominationally-specific programs would adjust emphases accordingly—for instance, adding courses on canon law, sacramental theology, or particular confessional standards.
