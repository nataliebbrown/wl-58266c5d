# Exploring Faith Curriculum

A comprehensive program for seekers, skeptics, and the spiritually curious who want to honestly investigate Christianity, its claims, its founder, and what it means to follow Jesus. This curriculum is designed to be intellectually rigorous, personally relevant, and genuinely open to questions.

---

## Introduction: Before You Begin

This curriculum assumes nothing about your current beliefs. You may be:

- An atheist or agnostic curious about what Christians actually believe
- Spiritual but not religious, exploring various traditions
- From another faith background wanting to understand Christianity
- A former Christian reconsidering faith after deconstruction
- Simply curious about Jesus and his lasting influence
- Skeptical but open to honest investigation
- Searching for meaning, purpose, or answers to life's big questions

Whatever brings you here, this curriculum invites you to explore with intellectual honesty, genuine curiosity, and personal reflection. You are encouraged to ask hard questions, voice doubts, and engage critically. The goal is not to pressure you toward any conclusion, but to help you understand Christianity deeply enough to make an informed decision about its claims.

## A Note on Honesty

This curriculum is written from within the Christian tradition, but with genuine respect for your autonomy and intelligence. The goal is not manipulation or pressure, but honest presentation of what Christianity claims, the evidence for and against it, and the implications of believing or not believing.

You may conclude that Christianity is true and commit your life to following Jesus. You may conclude that it's false and walk away with greater clarity about what you do believe. You may remain uncertain and continue seeking. All of these are legitimate outcomes of honest exploration.

What matters is that you engage authentically, ask your real questions, consider the evidence fairly, and make a decision that reflects your genuine convictions—not social pressure, emotional manipulation, or intellectual dishonesty from any direction.

The invitation of Jesus was simple: "Come and see" (John 1:39). This curriculum is an extended opportunity to do exactly that.

## If You Decide to Believe

If through this exploration you come to faith in Jesus Christ, here are suggested next steps:

1. **Tell Someone** – Share your decision with a Christian friend or pastor
2. **Get Connected** – Find a healthy church community
3. **Be Baptized** – Public declaration of your new faith
4. **Begin Learning** – Move to the "Foundations of Christian Faith" curriculum for new believers
5. **Start Practicing** – Begin prayer, Bible reading, and Christian community
6. **Expect Questions** – Conversion is a beginning, not an ending; more questions will come

---

## If You Decide Not to Believe

If you conclude that Christianity is not true, we respect your conclusion and offer these thoughts:

1. **Keep Seeking** – The big questions don't go away; keep pursuing truth wherever it leads
2. **Stay Open** – Many have returned to faith later in life; leave the door open
3. **Maintain Relationships** – Don't let this exploration damage relationships with Christians who care about you
4. **Live Consistently** – Whatever worldview you adopt, pursue it with integrity
5. **Revisit Occasionally** – Life circumstances change; it may be worth exploring again someday
