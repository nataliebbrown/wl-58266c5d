# Foundations of Christian Faith Curriculum

A comprehensive program for new believers beginning their journey in the Christian faith. This curriculum is designed to build a solid biblical and theological foundation while nurturing spiritual growth and community involvement.

---

## Phase One: First Steps (Months 1–3)

### Core Foundations

- **What Is Christianity?** – An overview of the Christian faith, its history, and core beliefs
- **Who Is Jesus?** – The life, teachings, death, and resurrection of Christ
- **Understanding the Gospel** – The good news of salvation explained simply
- **Assurance of Salvation** – Knowing and understanding your new relationship with God
- **The Bible: An Introduction** – What the Bible is, how it's organized, and why it matters

### Beginning Your Walk

- **How to Read the Bible** – Practical tools for daily Scripture reading
- **Learning to Pray** – The basics of communicating with God
- **What Happens When I Sin?** – Confession, repentance, and grace
- **Finding a Church Home** – Why community matters and how to choose a church
- **Water Baptism** – Understanding and preparing for this step of obedience

---

## Phase Two: Building Blocks (Months 4–6)

### Knowing God

- **The Character of God** – Who God is and what He is like
- **God as Father** – Understanding your adoption into God's family
- **Jesus as Savior and Lord** – Deepening your understanding of Christ's work
- **The Holy Spirit** – Who He is and how He works in your life
- **The Trinity Explained** – One God in three persons

### Knowing Yourself in Christ

- **Your New Identity** – Who you are now as a child of God
- **Freedom from Shame and Guilt** – Living in the reality of forgiveness
- **Understanding Grace** – Unmerited favor and its implications for daily life
- **Spiritual Warfare Basics** – Recognizing the enemy and standing firm
- **Renewing Your Mind** – Transforming thought patterns through Scripture

### Growing in Practice

- **Worship as a Lifestyle** – More than singing—a whole-life response to God
- **Generosity and Stewardship** – Managing money, time, and resources God's way
- **Sabbath and Rest** – The rhythm of work and renewal
- **Fasting: An Introduction** – The purpose and practice of fasting

---

## Phase Three: Deepening Roots (Months 7–9)

### Old Testament Foundations

- **The Story of the Old Testament** – Creation to the prophets in overview
- **Genesis: Beginnings** – Creation, fall, flood, and the patriarchs
- **Exodus and Redemption** – Israel's deliverance as a picture of salvation
- **The Law and the Christian** – Understanding the Ten Commandments and their relevance today
- **Psalms for Daily Life** – Using the Psalms in prayer and worship
- **Proverbs: Wisdom for Living** – Practical guidance for everyday decisions

### New Testament Foundations

- **The Story of the New Testament** – Gospels through Revelation in overview
- **The Gospel of Mark** – A fast-paced introduction to Jesus' ministry
- **The Gospel of John** – Jesus revealed as the Son of God
- **Acts: The Birth of the Church** – How the early church began and grew
- **Romans: The Gospel Explained** – Paul's masterful presentation of salvation
- **James: Faith That Works** – Practical Christianity in action

---

## Phase Four: Living It Out (Months 10–12)

### Christian Character and Virtue

- **The Fruit of the Spirit** – Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control
- **Humility and Servanthood** – Following Jesus' example
- **Integrity and Honesty** – Living transparently before God and others
- **Forgiveness: Giving and Receiving** – The hard work of letting go
- **Purity in Thought and Action** – Honoring God with your body and mind
- **Contentment and Gratitude** – Finding satisfaction in Christ alone

### Faith in Relationships

- **Honoring Your Family** – Faith in the context of home
- **Christian Friendship** – Building godly community
- **Navigating Difficult Relationships** – Boundaries, reconciliation, and wisdom
- **Sharing Your Faith** – How to naturally talk about Jesus with others
- **Loving Your Neighbor** – Practical compassion and service

### Faith in the World

- **Christianity and Work** – Bringing faith into your vocation
- **Christianity and Culture** – Engaging the world thoughtfully
- **Suffering and the Christian Life** – Why bad things happen and how to respond
- **Money, Possessions, and the Kingdom** – A biblical perspective on wealth
- **Justice, Mercy, and the Poor** – God's heart for the vulnerable

---

## Phase Five: Going Deeper (Year Two and Beyond)

### Spiritual Disciplines

- **Solitude and Silence** – Meeting God in stillness
- **Scripture Meditation** – Moving beyond reading to dwelling in the Word
- **Intercessory Prayer** – Praying for others with purpose
- **Journaling as a Spiritual Practice** – Tracking your journey with God
- **Spiritual Retreat** – Extended times of renewal and focus
- **Practicing the Presence of God** – Cultivating awareness of God throughout the day

### Theological Literacy

- **Basic Doctrine Survey** – The essential beliefs of Christianity
- **Church History for Beginners** – How we got here from there
- **Understanding Different Denominations** – What unites and distinguishes Christian traditions
- **Creeds and Confessions** – The Apostles' Creed, Nicene Creed, and why they matter
- **How We Got the Bible** – Canon, translation, and transmission
- **Interpreting the Bible** – Basic principles for understanding Scripture correctly

### Ministry and Service

- **Discovering Your Spiritual Gifts** – Identifying how God has equipped you to serve
- **Serving in the Local Church** – Finding your place in the body of Christ
- **Introduction to Small Group Leadership** – Facilitating community and growth
- **Caring for Others** – Basic pastoral care and encouragement
- **Personal Evangelism** – Practical training in sharing the gospel
- **Introduction to Missions** – God's global purpose and your part in it

---

- **Faith and Singleness** – Following Jesus as an unmarried person
- **Preparing for Christian Marriage** – Foundations for a God-centered relationship
- **Parenting with Purpose** – Raising children in the faith
- **Faith in the Workplace** – Monday through Friday Christianity
- **Navigating Doubt** – Honest questions and the life of faith
- **Grief and Loss** – Walking through dark valleys with God
- **Addiction and Recovery** – Finding freedom through Christ and community
- **Mental Health and Faith** – Understanding anxiety, depression, and getting help

### Worldview and Apologetics

- **Why Believe?** – Reasons for faith in a skeptical age
- **Tough Questions** – Addressing common objections to Christianity
- **Science and Faith** – Understanding the relationship between the two
- **Christianity and Other Religions** – What makes Christianity distinct
- **Ethics for Everyday Life** – Making moral decisions as a Christian

### Specialized Bible Studies

- **The Sermon on the Mount** – Jesus' vision for kingdom living
- **The Parables of Jesus** – Stories that reveal the heart of God
- **Philippians: Joy in All Circumstances**
- **Ephesians: Life in Christ**
- **1 John: Assurance and Love**

## Practical Requirements

A well-rounded formation program should include:

- **Regular Church Attendance** – Weekly participation in corporate worship
- **Small Group Participation** – Ongoing community with other believers
- **One-on-One Mentorship** – A more mature Christian walking alongside you
- **Scripture Memory** – Committing key verses to heart
- **Service Project** – Hands-on ministry experience
- **Personal Testimony Development** – Learning to articulate your story of faith
- **Accountability Partnership** – A trusted friend for honesty and encouragement

## Notes

This curriculum is designed to be flexible and adaptable to individual needs, church contexts, and denominational traditions. The pacing can be adjusted based on the learner's background, availability, and spiritual hunger. The goal is not merely information but transformation—head knowledge leading to heart change and life application.

Many churches offer versions of these courses through:
- Sunday school classes
- Midweek Bible studies
- New believer classes
- Discipleship groups
- One-on-one mentoring relationships
- Online learning platforms

The most important element is not completing a curriculum but cultivating a living, growing relationship with Jesus Christ in the context of Christian community.
