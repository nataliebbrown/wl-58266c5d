# Scripture AI Onboarding Quiz: All Possible User Outcomes

## Mathematical Calculation

- Question 1: 5 options (Spiritual Background)
- Question 2: 5 options (Learning Style)
- Question 3: 5 options (Community Preference)
- Question 4: 6 options (Current Spiritual Season)

**Total Combinations: 5 × 5 × 5 × 6 = 750 possible outcomes**

## Question Options Reference

### Q1 - Spiritual Background:
- "I'm new to faith and the Bible" (NEW)
- "I've been a believer for years but want to go deeper" (DEEPER)
- "I'm a pastor/ministry leader looking for better tools" (LEADER)
- "I'm studying theology or in seminary" (STUDENT)
- "I'm exploring faith and have questions" (EXPLORING)

### Q2 - Learning Style:
- "I learn best by reading and reflection" (READ)
- "I'm a visual learner who loves maps, images, and diagrams" (VISUAL)
- "I learn through conversation and discussion" (DISCUSS)
- "I prefer hands-on, interactive experiences" (INTERACTIVE)
- "I like discovering connections and patterns" (PATTERNS)

### Q3 - Community Preference:
- "I prefer individual, personal study" (SOLO)
- "I'm part of a small group or Bible study" (GROUP)
- "I lead groups and want discussion/teaching aids" (FACILITATE)
- "I'm looking for spiritual community" (SEEKING)
- "I like both personal and group experiences" (MIXED)

### Q4 - Current Spiritual Season:
- "Seeking deeper relationship with God" (RELATIONSHIP)
- "Working through questions or doubts" (DOUBTS)
- "Walking with a difficult life situation" (CRISIS)
- "Preparing for ministry or leadership" (MINISTRY)
- "Wanting to understand the Bible better" (UNDERSTANDING)
- "Looking for spiritual growth and formation" (GROWTH)
